About

I am an ELLIS PhD candidate at Fraunhofer Heinrich Hertz Institute under Prof. Wojciech Samek, with a secondary affiliation at the University of Oxford, where I am co-supervised by Dr. Christian Schröder de Witt and Prof. Philip Torr. My current research is focused on AI for history: building machine-learning systems that help historians read, connect, and reason about historical sources at scale.

Previously I worked on mechanistic interpretability of multimodal foundation models, with a focus on typographic understanding. I co-founded Bliss e.V., a Berlin-based non-profit research collective, and remain a core contributor to the ViT-Prisma toolkit for vision and video interpretability.
